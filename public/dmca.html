How to send a notice

If you believe content on Festie infringes your copyright, send the agent a written notice. It must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you claim we infringe.
  3. A description of the infringing material and enough information for us to find it, for example the festival, set or artist page URL.
  4. Your contact information: name, address, phone number and email.
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act for the copyright owner.

Counter-notice

If we removed your material and you believe that was a mistake, send the agent a counter-notice. It must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. A description of the removed material and where it appeared before we removed it.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith that we removed the material by mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address and phone number.
  5. Your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the address you gave. Outside the United States, you consent to any judicial district where Festie may be found.
  6. Your consent to accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We send your counter-notice to the complainant. Festie may restore the material in 10 to 14 business days, unless the complainant tells us it has filed a court action.
