1. Service Description

Festie is a real-time festival schedule coordination platform that enables groups of users to collaborate on musical festival planning, including selecting performances, coordinating logistics, and sharing notes. The service is operated by Asir Khan and is self-hosted on-premise infrastructure.

Festie allows you to:

  • Create personal accounts and manage user profiles
  • Select and save festival performances, acts, and attractions
  • Add notes to your festival schedule
  • Create and participate in crew groups for collaborative planning
  • Coordinate schedules and share festival plans with crew members
  • Export your data in standard formats
