Delete your Festie account

In short

  • You can delete your account yourself, in the app or on the web. You do not need to email us.
  • Your account is hidden at once. We delete it for good 30 days later.
  • If you change your mind, sign in again within those 30 days and your account comes back.

Go to my account

If you are signed out, sign in first. Then open Account and scroll to the Danger zone.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Festie and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the Danger zone and choose Delete My Account.
  3. If you created a crew that has other members, choose who takes it over.
  4. Confirm with your password. If you sign in with Apple or Google and have no password, confirm by signing in with that provider again.

Delete your account on the web

  1. Go to festie.us/account and sign in. If signing in takes you to Home, open Account from the menu.
  2. Scroll to the Danger zone and choose Delete My Account.
  3. Choose who takes over any crew you created, then confirm as described above.

If you cannot sign in, email privacy@festie.us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We answer within 30 days.

What happens, and when

  • Right away: you are signed out everywhere, your devices stop getting notifications, and your festival plans are hidden from other people.
  • Within 30 days: if you sign in again, your account comes back as it was.
  • After 30 days: we delete your account and the data that belongs to it for good. This cannot be undone.

What we delete

  • Your account: username, email, password, date of birth, display name, photo and payment handles.
  • Your picks, notes, ratings and followed festivals.
  • Your place in every crew, including your status and activity in it.
  • Your signed-in devices, sessions and notification history.

A crew you created passes to its longest-standing member. If you were the only member, we delete the crew.

What stays

  • An expense another member paid and split with you stays in that crew's records, without your name.
  • Copies of our database backups that still hold your data are deleted on a rolling schedule. The Privacy Policy says how long that takes.

Want a copy of your data first?

In Account, under Your data, choose Download my data. You can download your data once a day.
